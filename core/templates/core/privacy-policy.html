Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This page explains what information MICA collects, why we collect it, who can see it, and what choices you have. MICA is run by University of Perpetual Help System DALTA — Las Piñas Campus for its students, and follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173).

In short

  • We store the details you give us when you sign up, plus what you enter in MICA: moods, diary notes, chats and check-in answers.
  • Guidance staff can see your mood levels and your check-in. They cannot see your diary notes or your Mica chats.
  • Every time a staff member opens your record, the system logs it.
  • Mica is an AI. It is not a counselor, and it is not watched around the clock.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Write to college.guidance@perpetualdalta.edu.ph.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, username, email, student number, course and year level. If you add them: nickname, contact number, emergency contact and profile photo.
  • Your entries: daily mood check-ins, optional diary notes, breathing exercise sessions and notification settings.
  • Your conversations: messages with Mica, and messages and appointments with the guidance office.
  • Your check-in (assessment): your answers about how you have been feeling, the conditions you tick, and whether you have asked for help before. We keep every time you retake it.
  • Records made by staff: notes from sessions, referrals and risk flags.
  • Sign-in and security records: your consent record, sign-in messages sent to your email (with the time and IP address), and failed password attempts, which we keep only for a short time.

2. Why we use it

  • To let you track your mood and look after yourself.
  • To give you supportive replies from Mica.
  • To help the guidance office notice students who may need support, and to reach out.
  • To schedule guidance services and keep their records.
  • To make counts for guidance reports, for example how many sessions a service delivered.
  • To keep the system secure.

3. Who can see what

  • You can see all of your own data.
  • Your diary notes are for you only. Staff cannot see them.
  • Your Mica chats are not shown to staff. If you turn on "share topics with guidance" in your settings, staff see short labels such as "Sleep" or "Academic stress", never your words. Turn it off and the labels are removed.
  • Guidance staff see different things depending on their role, which the System Admin sets. This can include your mood levels, your check-in, the guidance chat, and session notes. Staff decide who to reach out to. MICA may suggest a student for review, but nobody is flagged automatically.
  • The System Admin manages accounts and staff roles. The admin tools do not show your diary, chats or session notes.
  • Access log: when staff open your profile, chat, notes or check-in, MICA records who did it and when.
  • The project team that maintains the system can technically reach stored data through the database. This access is limited to [authorized maintainers].

4. Services we use to run MICA

  • Google Gemini: Mica's replies are made by Google's Gemini service. Your message and your recent conversation are sent to it to write a reply. If you share topics, parts of your recent messages are also sent to label them. Please do not put anything in a Mica chat that you do not want processed this way.
  • Firebase (Google): used for Google sign-in and for password reset codes. Your email address is stored there for this.
  • Email service: sends your sign-in and password reset messages.
  • Voice input: your recording is turned into text on our own server and is not kept.

These services have their own privacy policies.

5. How we keep it safe

  • Passwords are stored scrambled and cannot be read, even by us.
  • Each sign-in is confirmed with a code or link sent to your email.
  • Repeated wrong passwords lock the account for a short time.
  • You are signed out automatically after a period of no activity.
  • Staff can only use the parts of MICA their role allows.
  • Access to student records is logged.
  • The connection to our database is encrypted.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your records while you are a student. When an account is archived or you graduate, the guidance office keeps your records for [retention period]. MICA does not delete data automatically.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used,
  • see the data we hold about you and get a copy,
  • correct data that is wrong,
  • object to how your data is used, or withdraw your consent,
  • ask for your data to be deleted or blocked,
  • complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these, write to college.guidance@perpetudalta.edu.ph. We will reply within 7 days.
