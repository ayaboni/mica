Your privacy and consent

MICA is a student well-being app. Before you start, here is what we keep, who can see it, and your choices. This follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

What we keep

  • Your account details: name, student number, course, year level, email
  • Your mood check-ins and exercise usage statistics
  • Your chat topics with Mica (the AI companion)
  • Your messages and appointments with the Guidance Office
  • Your answers to the quick check-in questions

What Guidance can see

  • Your name, student number, course, and year level
  • Your mood check-in levels
  • Your messages and appointments with Guidance
  • Your Student Profile Data Sheet answers (shared with and kept by the Guidance Office, see below)

What stays private

  • Guidance staff cannot read your diary entries or your chat messages with Mica.
  • System administrators who maintain the database could technically access stored data. They should only do so for maintenance.

If someone is worried about you

If a counselor is worried about your well-being, they may note it internally and reach out to you. The AI never reports or flags you by itself.

Please remember

Mica is not a doctor or an emergency service. If you are in danger or thinking of hurting yourself, please contact someone right now:

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by contacting the Guidance Office or the system admin.
