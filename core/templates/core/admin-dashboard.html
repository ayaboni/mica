How MICA protects student data

What the system actually does today
  • Students must accept the consent form before using MICA.
  • Staff only see what their role allows (Roles & Permissions).
  • Every time staff open a student's record, it is logged in Audit Logs.
  • Sign-in: 5 wrong passwords lock the account for 60 seconds, and an e-mailed 6-character code (valid 10 minutes, 5 tries) is required.
  • Passwords need 8+ characters, 1 uppercase letter, and 1 number or symbol.
  • Idle sign-out: 15 minutes for staff and admin, 30 minutes for students.
  • Archived and graduated students cannot sign in, and nothing is deleted.

Good to know

Things the admin should be able to answer honestly
  • Mica chat messages are processed by Google Gemini. Firebase is used for Google sign-in and password-reset codes.
  • MICA does not automatically delete or anonymize old accounts.
  • MICA does not add its own encryption of stored data. Storage protection depends on the database host.
  • Data access, correction, and erasure requests are handled by the Guidance Office. The admin only logs them here, and nothing is changed or erased automatically.
  • Failed sign-in attempts (time, the account if it exists, and network address) are kept for 90 days to help spot attacks.

Sign-in and session rules

Set in the code, so they are shown here but not editable.
  • Passwords need 8+ characters, 1 uppercase letter, and 1 number or symbol.
  • 5 wrong passwords lock the account for 60 seconds. Many failures from one network address lock that address for a few minutes.
  • System Admin accounts also need a code from an authenticator app (or a one-time recovery code) every time they sign in.
  • E-mailed sign-in code: 6 characters, valid 10 minutes, 5 tries.
  • Idle sign-out: 15 minutes for staff and admin, 30 minutes for students.
  • New staff accounts have no password. They sign in with their username and an e-mailed code, then set one.
  • Archived and graduated students cannot sign in.
