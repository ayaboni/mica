Our Mission

Mental health among college students has become an increasingly important concern in recent years. The prevalence of mental-health-related issues continues to rise, affecting students' emotional well-being and academic success.

Mica is a web-based AI-assisted mental health monitoring and coaching platform designed to support students in managing their mental well-being. It provides mood tracking, guided breathing exercises, a private digital journal, and AI-driven support while ensuring the confidentiality of student information.

Mica aims to promote mental wellness by encouraging help-seeking behavior, providing insights into users' emotional well-being, and facilitating access to campus guidance services.

What Mica Offers

AI Chatbot

Talk to Mica about your day or your worries. Mica is an AI companion, so it is there whenever you open the app.

Connect with Guidance

Message the university's guidance office and schedule appointments, all in one place.

Mood Tracking and Diary

Check in daily with your mood and write freely in your private diary. Your diary notes are for your eyes only.

Calming Exercises

Small tools to help you slow down and reset:

  • 1-minute Breathing
  • Affirmations
  • 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding
  • Mica Pop
  • Pomodoro Timer
  • Daily Quests

What Mica Is, and Is Not

Mica is

  • A friendly AI companion you can talk to
  • A place to track your mood and write privately
  • A link to your campus guidance office
  • A set of tools to help you calm down and reset

Mica is not

  • A counselor, therapist, or doctor
  • A tool that diagnoses anything
  • Watched by a person around the clock
  • A replacement for talking to people you trust
