How risk levels are decided

Mood check-ins from the last 14 days. Needs at least 3 check-ins for the average rules.
LevelRuleSuggested follow-up
UrgentLast 3 check-ins are all 2 or below, OR the average is 2.0 or belowReview first. Consider a check-in message.
WatchAverage is 3.0 or below, OR no check-in for 5+ daysReview this week.
StableEverything elseNo action needed.
No dataNever checked inNothing to judge yet.

Confirm the follow-up timing with your guidance counselor. Going quiet is not the same as being in distress, which is why it only reaches Watch.

Mood score key

  • 5: Great
  • 4: Good
  • 3: Okay
  • 2: Low
  • 1: Struggling

Used: mood check-in levels only.
Never used: diary text, Mica chats, check-in answers.
